FAQ

What types of properties does HouseThat sell?

HouseThat specializes in selling premium residential and commercial properties including apartments, independent houses, villas, shops, and commercial spaces. We work as an Authorised Channel Partner for verified real estate projects.

How can I view a property before purchasing?

You can schedule a property viewing by contacting our sales team. Simply call us at +91 9696968030 or +91 7840803090, or fill out the contact form on this page. Our team will arrange a convenient time for you to visit the property.

What documents are required to buy a property?

You'll need identity proof (Aadhaar card/PAN card), address proof, passport-sized photographs, income proof, and a cancelled cheque. For loan applications, additional financial documents may be required. Our team will guide you through the complete documentation process.

What payment plans and financing options are available?

We offer flexible payment plans including construction-linked plans and down payment options. We also assist with home loan approvals from leading banks and financial institutions. Contact our sales team to discuss the best payment plan suitable for your budget.

When will I get possession of the property?

Possession timelines vary based on the project and construction status. Some properties are ready-to-move while others may have specific possession dates. Our sales team will provide detailed information about possession dates and project timelines for each property.

Are the properties RERA registered?

Yes, HouseThat is a RERA registered Channel Partner (UPRERAAGT13108). We deal only with RERA-approved projects to ensure legal compliance and buyer protection. All properties we sell are verified and legally compliant.

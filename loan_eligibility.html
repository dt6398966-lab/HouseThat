About Eligibility Calculator

Know how a bank provides a deal for their customers

Lenders have drafted a set of parameters for borrowers that are to be fulfilled while availing for a home loan. Home loan eligibility criteria include age, income, work experience, and credit history.

Lenders have drafted a set of parameters for borrowers that are to be fulfilled while availing for a home loan. Home loan eligibility criteria include age, income, work experience, and credit history.

To determine your eligibility, lenders typically consider:

  • Age: Most lenders require applicants to be between 18-70 years old.
  • Income: Your monthly or annual income determines your repayment capacity.
  • Occupation: Salaried, self-employed, or business professionals have different eligibility criteria.
  • Existing EMIs: Your current loan commitments affect your borrowing capacity.
  • Credit Score: A good credit history improves your loan eligibility.

Frequently asked questions about Eligibility Calculator

Know what questions the users frequently asks about Eligibility calculators.

What are the eligibility criteria for a home loan?
Home loan eligibility criteria typically include: minimum age of 18 years, maximum age of 70 years at loan maturity, minimum income requirements (varies by lender and city), stable employment or business income, good credit score (usually 750+), and existing financial obligations should not exceed 40-50% of income.
How is eligibility for home loan calculated?
Home loan eligibility is calculated based on your monthly income, existing EMI commitments, age, occupation, and credit history. The general formula used is: Eligible Loan Amount = (Monthly Income × 60) - (Existing EMIs × Remaining Tenure). Lenders typically consider 40-60% of your income as available for EMI payments.
What is the minimum salary for home loan?
The minimum salary requirement for a home loan varies by lender, city, and loan amount. Generally, for metro cities, a minimum monthly income of ₹25,000-₹30,000 is required. For tier-2 cities, it may be ₹20,000-₹25,000. Self-employed individuals need to show consistent income through IT returns and bank statements.
What are the eligibility requirements for an NRI seeking a home loan?
NRIs can avail home loans in India with the following requirements: valid passport and visa, work permit in the country of residence, minimum age of 18 years, stable income abroad, NRE/NRO bank account in India, power of attorney if property is being purchased by a relative, and property should be for self-use or renting (not for investment).
Can I get a home loan if I have existing loans?
Yes, you can get a home loan even if you have existing loans. However, lenders will consider your total EMI burden (existing EMIs + proposed home loan EMI). Generally, your total EMI should not exceed 40-50% of your monthly income. Having a good credit history and repayment track record for existing loans improves your eligibility.
How does credit score affect loan eligibility?
Credit score significantly impacts your loan eligibility. A score above 750 is considered excellent and helps you get better interest rates and higher loan amounts. Scores between 650-750 are acceptable but may result in higher interest rates. Scores below 650 may lead to rejection or very high interest rates. Maintaining a good credit history is crucial for loan approval.
What documents are required for home loan eligibility check?
For salaried individuals: Identity proof (Aadhaar, PAN, Passport), address proof, salary slips (last 3-6 months), Form 16, bank statements (last 6 months), and employment certificate. For self-employed: IT returns (last 2-3 years), balance sheet and P&L statement, bank statements, business registration documents, and GST certificate if applicable.
Can I increase my loan eligibility?
Yes, you can increase your loan eligibility by: adding a co-applicant (spouse or family member with income), clearing existing loans to reduce EMI burden, maintaining a good credit score (750+), showing additional income sources, opting for longer tenure, choosing a property in a recognized location, and keeping a good relationship with your bank through savings accounts or other financial products.
What is the difference between loan eligibility and loan sanction?
Loan eligibility is a preliminary assessment based on your income and financial profile, giving an approximate loan amount you may be eligible for. Loan sanction is the formal approval after detailed verification of documents, property valuation, and credit assessment. Eligibility is just an estimate, while sanction is the actual approved loan amount that you can avail.

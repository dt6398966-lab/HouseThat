Festive fervour drives the homebuying sentiment

#Property Punditz #Purchase #Residential
2 min read Sep 24, 2021

By HouseThat

Real Estate Knowledge Platform

319 Views
Share:

Property purchases are the most common during the festive season. While some relate the festive period with good luck, some see it as the perfect time to crack a deal as many real estate developers offer discounts and freebies.

Festivals in India are rich in mythological stories, and every festival has a tale attached to it. Here, the festivals are marked by novelty and auspiciousness. Hence, the occasion of a festival is considered the perfect time to start something new or invest in something new. In the same vein, merchants and businesses alike run the craziest and biggest deals to boost their sales and please customers. It is believed that there are no bad omens during this period, thus many people plan their home purchases during this 'shubhmuhurat'.

Festive seasons drive significant property sales in India

Therefore, it would not be wrong to say that the Indian property market remains sentiment-driven, and the festive season has always been considered auspicious for home buying. The festive fervour combined with deal sweeteners and festive bonanzas allures potential homebuyers to pursue a transaction.

Additionally, the COVID-19 pandemic has underpinned the value of owning a house, and as a result, the market has witnessed a pent-up demand. The safety and security that a home offers are imperative to drive demand in conducive market conditions with decadal low home loan interest rates and innovative flexi payment schemes to cushion the home financing.

The industry seemed well-equipped to handle the second wave of the COVID-19 with ongoing construction work along with in-situ labours expedited vaccination drive following the COVID-19 norms and resumption of economic activities. This has led to positive sectoral growth with record-high property sales leading to a drop in unsold inventory and debt refinancing. This liquidity cushion will nudge branded developers to launch new projects across market segments and cater to affordable, mid and luxury housing demand.

Moreover, the demand from first time home buyers is increasing due to the significance of the owned house. In contrast, the new normal lifestyle has triggered the need for upgrading to the larger spacious home. The change in consumer taste and preference amid the pandemic will result in an uptick in sustainable housing demand during a festive mood.

The digitisation and technology integration in the pre-purchase research process of home buying also saves time and cost and fast tracks the sale closure of one's dream home. Further, the rise in employment amid geopolitical uncertainties and pay hikes will also push fence-sitters to buy or invest in the property market in festive tailwinds.

Lastly, as the threat of the pandemic still hangs in the air, offers and deals will be a major factor in determining how successful the festive sales season will be for the realty sector. Developers will rely more heavily on pushing offers aggressively to attract new customers and acquire traction as a result of the pandemic. Hence, the festive season will bolster the housing demand, and the property market will reflect green shoot recovery after a prolonged sluggishness.
